# DC-02 — Fee-clearance policy enforced when issuing new TC

**Source:** Client Chat item 2; connects PDF clarification 4 (leaving/TC lifecycle). **Status:** [tracker](../TASK-TRACKER.md). **Existing code:** platform-admin TC issue currently has no authoritative unpaid-fee guard. **Scope:** proposed pending client approval of fee categories/as-of/override policy.

## Requirement and use cases

A new TC cannot be issued by either full operator or platform admin when the student has **relevant** unpaid school fees, including previous-month and previous-session arrears as decided by the client. The amount and a safe breakdown must be communicated to the authorized user.

- **UC-01:** Fee ₹1 pending, issue TC -> 409 business error, no TC row/job/file created.
- **UC-02:** August partial ₹500 and September ₹700 -> clearance sees ₹1,200 via shared fees statement.
- **UC-03:** Full settlement -> issue succeeds on retry, frozen clearance evidence recorded.
- **UC-04:** Paid receipt later voided -> future issue blocks; existing valid TC is not retroactively deleted.
- **UC-05:** Platform-admin direct POST, operator direct POST and background job path cannot bypass the check.
- **UC-06:** Fee waiver/exemption only if a client-approved adjusted ledger entry exists; no “ignore dues” UI switch.

## Proposed DLD

**Owner/boundaries:** TC issuer remains in document/TC owner and calls a new public `FeesPublicFacade.checkDocumentClearance`, without querying FeeCharge or FeeDue directly. Fee domain is only authority for charge eligibility, waivers, partial payments, opening balances and arrears.

**Contract:** `checkDocumentClearance({schoolId,studentId,purpose:'TC',asOfDate,policyVersion,academicSessionId?})` returns `{eligible:boolean, duePaise:string, outstanding:[{chargeId,period,category,balancePaise}],ledgerRevision,computedAt,policyVersion}`. All money Decimal or minor-unit strings, not JavaScript float. Missing/unassessed fee months are separately flagged and must not be misrepresented as confirmed debt zero. Differentiate outstanding >0 vs missing rule.

**DB:** Store issue-time `DocumentClearanceDecision` record or immutable clearance hash/ledger revision in TC issue encrypted snapshot: schoolId, studentId, decision, amount, policy version, check timestamp, safe charge IDs; no sensitive identity. Index by school/student/issuedAt. Do not edit existing historical TC snapshots.

**Transactions/concurrency:** Before TC/job insertion, check clearance, lock or verify ledger version within the same gated issuance commit; on race with assessment/payment/void, recheck and fail closed on changed ledger. Issue row, Job row and audit outbox created atomically after eligibility. If architectural services cannot share transaction, define a write-serialized clearance reservation or explicit revision/optimistic validation: no stale passed result. Use idempotency key for retries of a request.

**API:** Shared internal `TcIssue` called by platform and operator controllers; return `409 ERR_FEES_PENDING_FOR_TC` with sanitized outstanding count/total and linkable student id, not Aadhaar. Other errors: `403` role denial, `404` cross-school student/TC, `422` invalid policy context, `503` unavailable clearance dependency. Operator UI shows 'Pending fees' and View Fees link. Disabled button is convenience, not security.

**Business decisions:** Confirm whether old session debts and books/uniform/transport/late/exam are blockers; whether issue cutoff is date; any exceptional authorized overrides; what to do when months have not been assessed; whether issuance automatically leaves school (CL-01). Until confirmed do not silently implement any policy.
