# CL-06 — Receipt and Student ID/SR numbering policies across years

**Source:** PDF clarification 9 (page 4); previously omitted.. **Work status:** [single tracker](../TASK-TRACKER.md). **Approval required:** update Harness authority before implementation. **Current code / owner:** receipt-number-allocator.ts, student-identifier-allocator.ts, ReceiptSequence, StudentIdSequence models.

## Business goal, concrete use cases and acceptance
**User story:** As school administrator, I can follow the school's agreed numbering sequence across academic and financial years while never changing an already issued student's identity or receipt.

**Use cases and expected results:** UC1: continuous receipt series increments beyond 2027; UC2: academic-year scoped series restarts at 00001 but prefix prevents collision; UC3: financial-year series uses Apr–Mar date boundaries; UC4: student auto-ID rolls over only if configured, never collides with existing studentCode; UC5: old printed documents keep original number after policy changes.

**Business policy and acceptance rules:** Policy selection must be explicit: CONTINUOUS / ACADEMIC_YEAR / FINANCIAL_YEAR for receipts and separately for Student ID/SR; exact printed prefix, width and reset sequence are client decisions. Internal immutable UUID and `idempotencyKey` remain stable. No reuse of historical receipt numbers in same display scope; prevent current Student ID collision even after year reset.

## Detailed Level Design (proposed until approved)
**DB schema, indexes and migration/backfill:** SchoolNumberingPolicy(schoolId,entityType RECEIPT|STUDENT_ID,seriesRule,prefixPattern,width,version,effectiveFrom,changedBy); NumberSequence(schoolId,entityType,seriesKey,lastValue) UNIQUE triple; receipt has frozen seriesKey, formattedReceiptNumber with unique school-scoped constraint; student identifier history continues current immutable technical FK. Index lookup for formatted numbers and history.

**API/routes/contracts, validation and authorization:** GET/PATCH /platform/schools/:id/numbering-policies (platform owner or delegated school admin by approved role) {entityType,seriesRule,prefixPattern,width,version}; GET /operator/fees/numbering-preview?sessionId; student ID suggestion returns advisory only, allocator reserves during student create transaction.

**Frontend screens, backend service orchestration, ports/facades and processing algorithm:** Sequence service obtains per-school+entity+series FOR UPDATE, computes seriesKey from selected session or Asia/Kolkata financial year, applies validated template then commits with payment/student transaction and audit. Web setup shows example 2026 vs 2027 outputs and warns that existing historical identifiers do not change. Legacy ReceiptSequence and StudentIdSequence migrated to CONTINUOUS policy with seeded lastValue, avoiding reissue.

**Errors, edge cases, reconciliation and recovery design:** If session year and financial year overlap, explicit policy wins. Multiple schools can share formatted receipt number only because school tenant scope differentiates them; in-school duplicate forbidden. Prefix edits cannot collide with old numbers; validate uniqueness on change.
