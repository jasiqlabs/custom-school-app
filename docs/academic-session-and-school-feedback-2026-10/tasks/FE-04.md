# FE-04 — Optional Books and Uniform fees during admission only

**Evidence:** Chat items 6 and 7; no independent inventory module. **Execution:** [TASK-TRACKER](../TASK-TRACKER.md). **Authority:** Proposed only until PRD/HLD/story/UIUX/DLD gates approved. **Current touch points:** student admission form and service; FE-01 rule config; FE-07 charge ledger.

## Requirement, actors, real-world use cases
**Business story:** School operator selects Books and/or Uniform during admission, can show their cost in fee statement, without tracking stock, quantities or suppliers unless separately approved.

**Use cases and acceptance examples:** UC1: selects Books only, preview and created dues show Books charge. UC2: selects Books+Uniform, two independent fees; UC3: deselect both, no charges. UC4: student cannot trigger extra fee by submitting a fake low amount; server recalculates from rule. UC5: correct error without duplicate student after network timeout.

**Business invariants, eligibility, scope and denial conditions:** No Books/Uniform inventory, distribution, purchasing, vendor or stock dashboard. Only optional charge selection and posting at admission. Whether items are per-school per-session or one-time and whether amount override allowed must be explicitly decided.

## Detailed design: domain, schema, interfaces and UI
**Database schema / constraints / existing-data migration:** FeeCharge sourceKey school+student+category+admissionEventID; reference `FeeRuleId` and snapshot amount, no product SKU or inventory table. AdmissionSelection snapshot for audit records chosen category codes, not a permanent inventory entitlement.

**Exact proposed endpoint contract, input/output, role/tenant validation:** Student POST admission accepts optional `chargeSelections: ['BOOKS','UNIFORM']` only (or rule IDs validated school-side); GET /operator/students/admission-fee-options?classId&academicSessionId supplies configured amount and labels; server posts charges via Fees facade.

**Service algorithm, module ownership and external integration / UI flow:** Admission wizard section toggles with labeled configured prices; live review shows tuition + initial charges and any transport selection separately. On confirm, Students service validates school/class/session and requests FeesPublicFacade.postInitialCharges with source event; creates independent charges as configured; show receipt only after actual payment.

**Concurrency, edge cases, failure handling and idempotency:** Changing selection after paid billing needs adjustment/refund business rule, not deleting historical fee. Do not infer books delivered from fee paid. More than one admission in different year obeys approved one-time policy.
