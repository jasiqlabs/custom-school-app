# FE-05 — Previous-month arrears in current collection without double billing

**Trace to source:** Chat item 5; PDF clarification 5 regarding start/stop.. **State and owner:** [TASK-TRACKER](../TASK-TRACKER.md) is authoritative. **Baseline files:** query-pending-fees.service.ts, generate-dues.service.ts ensureDuesForApplicableMonths, collect UI, FeeStatement facade. **No feature implementation implied by planning completion.**

## 1. Detailed requirement and use-case definition
**User story:** At the time of collecting September fees, operator sees unpaid August balance in the same view and may settle both while each liability remains tied to its original billing month.

**Real school scenarios:** UC1: August ₹700, paid ₹200 -> August ₹500; September ₹700 -> total due ₹1,200. UC2: collect ₹600 under approved oldest-first policy -> August ₹0 and Sept ₹600; new payment allocations total ₹600. UC3: May fee from previous academic year remains outstanding and visible. UC4: void previously collected payment restores correct source month balance. UC5: missing May/June historical due because generation wasn't run shows 'unassessed' vs '0 owed' until controlled generation.

**Rules/eligibility/out-of-scope decisions:** Never duplicate an old debt as a current-month charge; carry forward is computed presentation. Which months to assess before product go-live is policy-controlled CL-02/03; do not automatically backcharge all past months. Oldest-first suggested but client can choose manual allocation. Ensure one statement source for pending reports, TC clearance and user UI.

## 2. Detailed Level Design (proposed until approved by Harness)
**Persistent data model, uniqueness, indexing, migration:** FeeCharge unique sourceKey school/student/category/period/event, originSessionId and assessedAt/dueDate. Outstanding computed from netAmount and non-void allocations (plus FeeAdjustment). Create indexed statement read projection by school/student/dueDate/status. Legacy FeeDue backfill under FE-07 compares ledgers; missing months repair via approval-driven idempotent assessment batch.

**REST/port contract, actor authorization, DTOs and error semantics:** GET /operator/fees/students/:studentId/statement?asOfDate&sessionId gives currentCharges, priorMonthArrears, priorSessionArrears, totalDue, unassessedPeriods; POST /operator/fees/payments accepts explicit list of allocation amounts to distinct charges. Pending report adds previousMonthsOutstanding and original periods while old month report stays compatible.

**Backend workflow, module responsibilities, frontend states and integration strategy:** Fees domain computes all eligible outstanding charges (including older sessions), sorts by dueDate, server calculates aggregates using Decimal and response with per-line IDs. UI has previous-month arrears grouped by month and current charge list with checkboxes; payment preview cannot allocate more than original balance; after successful post refetch entire statement. TC clearance consumes same service, never separately sums fee_due table.

**Race conditions, failure cases, transaction/retry/rollback semantics:** Future-dated fee should not be reported already overdue, but may be payable early if policy; arrears as of cutoff defined. Leaving stops new fees, not collections. Credit/concession adjustments never silently erase old balances.
